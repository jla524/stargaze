Each row is a figure this site already records, tied to the date it was stated. A later note that repeats an older number does not replace a newer one. A target year is not a launch slot. An application is not permission to launch, and it is not a spacecraft design.

Checked against the [news log](/news/) through 1 October 2026. Whether a system has flown, is on a manifest, or is only a filing is on the [status board](/status/).

## How to read this

Use the newest block in a section when a design was revised. The older blocks stay so the numbers are not averaged. Where this site has no mass, power, or radiator figure, the sheet says so.

## AI1 / Starmind

[SpaceX](/companies/#spacex). Nothing with this name has flown. The spacecraft design and the constellation application are separate.

The design to use is the 16 September 2026 set. A 16 July 2026 note changed the power budget only. The 8 June 2026 note is the earlier full description. A 25 August 2026 article is later than July and still quotes the June power and wingspan.

**Current public design, 16 September 2026.** Ex parte filing in the Starmind docket. It does not restate an orbital altitude. The June disclosure said roughly 600 km. The application band is 500–2,000 km.

**Schedule.** Company statements, not a manifest.

- Late 2027 for the first AI1 satellites, with compute on Starlink satellites before that as canaries. Gwynne Shotwell, at the 2026 IPO. [Jun 12, 2026](/news/spacex-ipo-raises-75b-at-1-77t-valuation-orbital-ai-central-to-story/).
- A space-optimized Vera Rubin NVL72 aimed at the fourth quarter of 2027, and significant scale in 2028. [Aug 25, 2026](/news/spacex-and-nvidia-target-q4-2027-starmind-launch-scale-in-2028/). Both dates depend on Starship's flight rate.

**Application.** Not an authorization, and not the spacecraft table above.

- Up to one million satellites, 500–2,000 km, optical inter-satellite links. That post also states a 100 GW compute target. [Jan 30, 2026](/news/spacex-fcc-filing-for-1m-satellite-data-centers/).
- As of [Sept 16, 2026](/news/spacex-fcc-filing-puts-starmind-mass-at-4-tonnes/), the application has been accepted for filing and has not been authorized. Environmental groups have asked for a full NEPA environmental impact statement.

**Starcloud-3, announced.** About 200 kW, described on [March 30, 2026](/news/starcloud-raises-170-million-series-a-to-build-data-centers-in-space/) as a Starship-deployed spacecraft. As of [Aug 25, 2026](/news/starcloud-closes-250m-series-a-extension-at-2-3b-valuation/), production lines were being built, and there is still no public launch date for the spacecraft. That note keeps a Space-1 Vera Rubin module targeted for late 2028. The module date is not a spacecraft launch.

The module itself, not the spacecraft, is described as up to 25× the AI compute of an H100, with the module coming later. [Mar 2026](/news/nvidia-launches-space-computing-rocketing-ai-into-orbit/).

**SC-1L, manifested.** An AI compute payload on Firefly Elytra, in lunar orbit, after the tug finishes Blue Ghost relay duties. No earlier than 2028, on Firefly's third lunar mission. The note says Elytra then remains in lunar orbit for five years. This site has no mass or kilowatt figure for the payload. [Sept 30, 2026](/news/starcloud-books-lunar-orbit-compute-demo-on-firefly-elytra/).
