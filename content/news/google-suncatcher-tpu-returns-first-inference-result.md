Google asked the onboard model for a haiku. The downlink was:

> Blue marble below
> Data streams in silent grace
> Starlight feeds my core.

Travis Beals, senior director of Paradigms of Intelligence, called the exchange a hello-world milestone on the way to the 2027 pair of satellites.
