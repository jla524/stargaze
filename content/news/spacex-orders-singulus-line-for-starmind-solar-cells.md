The Starmind attribution is in [SINGULUS's same-day statement](https://www.singulus.com/2026/11/10/spacex-heterojunction-solar-cells-singulus/). Nicolls called the contract a manufacturing path for solar cells for Starmind satellites at the Texas facility, and a durable option for scaling solar production.
